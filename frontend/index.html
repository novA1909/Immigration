Our Story

A Family Tradition Since 1985

When Mama Nia first arrived from Naples with nothing but her grandmother's recipes and a dream, she never imagined her little pizzeria would become a beloved institution.

Every morning, just as the sun rises, our pizzaiolos begin their ritual – mixing flour, water, and a touch of magic to create the perfect dough. It's the same recipe Mama Nia brought from the old country, unchanged for three generations.

Today, her grandchildren continue the legacy, honoring every pizza that leaves our kitchen with the same love and dedication that started it all.
